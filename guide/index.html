Dockly

Tap. Ride. Arrive. — On-demand boat rides in Fort Lauderdale. Pilot build for review.

This is a working prototype of the Dockly platform: a passenger app, a captain app and an operations panel, all running on a test database with Fort Lauderdale docks, canals and prices. Nothing here is public yet and no real payments or SMS are involved.

A 10-minute test drive

  1. Captain side first. Open the captain app, sign in as the demo captain, fill the registration form (any boat name, registration like FL 1234 AB, pick the waters you work). Submit.
  2. Approve the captain. Open the operations panel, section Captains, filter Pending, approve. Then in the captain app tap Go online.
  3. Request a ride. In the passenger app choose a departure dock (try Las Olas Marina) and a destination (Riverwalk · Esplanade Park or Shooters Waterfront). You get an instant price. Confirm.
  4. Accept it. The captain app shows the request with the route. Accept, then follow the steps: On my way → Arrived → Passenger on board → Done. The passenger sees the boat move on the map (on a phone, with location on).
  5. Try a counteroffer. On a new request, as captain tap Propose another price. The passenger can accept or decline.
  6. Look at the panel. Live shows the trip, Trips the history and receipt, Audit who did what.

What we would love to hear from you

  • Are the docks the right ones? Which marinas and restaurants would actually let a charter captain pick up and drop off?
  • Do the prices feel right for Fort Lauderdale? Today: $25 base + about $8 per mile, flat fares between the main docks.
  • What would a licensed captain need to see before signing up? (We plan to ask for the USCG license, insurance and drug-program enrollment.)
  • Anything confusing in the flow, in English, or on your phone screen.

Known limits of this build

  • Payment by card is not connected yet. Choose Cash when requesting.
  • Canal routes come from OpenStreetMap; a few side canals may be missing, in which case the price falls back to a distance estimate.
  • Web only for now. Native iOS and Android builds come after the pilot.
